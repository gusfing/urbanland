# Urbanland Design & Content Guidelines

This document serves as the complete guideline for replicating the Urbanland site on any new platform. It contains the exact brand colors, typography, and the complete homepage content copy.

---

## 1. Brand Guidelines & Aesthetics

### Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Outfit`, sans-serif
- **Secondary/System Fonts:** `Inter`, `Sora`, `Manrope` (mostly overriding with `Outfit` in the current theme)

### Color Palette
Use these exact hex codes for styling your new site to match the Urbanland theme perfectly.

**Base Colors:**
- **Background:** `#f4f4f4` (Light Gray/Off-White)
- **Foreground (Text):** `#1a1a1a` (Almost Black)

**Brand / Theme Colors:**
- **Primary:** `#2C5F2E` (Forest Green)
- **Primary Foreground:** `#F7F4EF`
- **Secondary:** `#2D2D2D` (Dark Charcoal)
- **Secondary Foreground:** `#F7F4EF`
- **Accent (Highlights/Buttons):** `#C9A84C` (Gold)
- **Accent Foreground:** `#1A1A1A`
- **Destructive / Alert:** `#8c3721` (Rust/Orange)
- **Muted Background:** `#F7F4EF` (Cream/Off-White)

**Gradients:**
- Primary dark gradient: `linear-gradient(#14743a 0%, #010101 100%)`
- Accent gradient: `linear-gradient(#8c3721 0%, #14743a 100%)`
